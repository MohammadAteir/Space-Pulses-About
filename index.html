Mission Context & Problem Statement

NASA’s 5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight

Long-duration missions like NASA’s Artemis program and future Mars journeys expose astronauts to five major space hazards where communication delays prevent real-time Earth support.

HAZARD 01 • SPACE RADIATION 01 / 05

Explored Reading Ahead
HAZARD 01

Space Radiation

Galactic cosmic rays and solar particle events increase DNA damage, radiation sickness, and long-term cardiovascular and cancer risks.

HAZARD 02

Isolation & Confinement

Months in small, closed spacecraft disrupt circadian rhythms, sleep hours, mood, and behavioral health.

HAZARD 03

Distance from Earth

Up to 22-minute signal delays prevent live Earth medical guidance or fast evacuation—crews need autonomous care onboard.

HAZARD 04

Altered Gravity Fields

Microgravity and partial lunar/Mars gravity trigger bone density loss, muscle atrophy, and fluid shifts toward the head.

HAZARD 05

Hostile Closed Environments

Recycled cabin air, elevated CO2, altered microbes, and lack of sunlight strain immunity and respiratory health.

Resulting Astronaut Health Risks

Combined exposure to these 5 hazards puts crews at high risk for:

Immune System Changes Heightened inflammation and weakened resistance in closed cabins.
Bone Density Loss & Cardiovascular Events 1–1.5% monthly skeletal loss, arrhythmias, and blood pressure shifts.
Inadequate Nutrition & Behavioral Health Issues Vitamin D depletion, metabolic changes, stress, and sleep disruption.

Spacecraft Energy Conservation

Smart Wake-on-Demand Architecture

To conserve critical spacecraft power, the display stays dormant in a 0.4W standby state until woken instantaneously by one of two smart modes.

SYSTEM POWER STATE
DORMANT STANDBY (0.4W) — CONSERVING ENERGY
Mode 01 // Biometric Trigger Smartwatch Wearable

Vital Anomaly Wake-Up

Connected to a wrist-worn wearable sensor device (smartwatch form factor) that tracks vitals continuously. The dashboard automatically wakes up and alerts the astronaut if any vital drops below or exceeds safe thresholds.

WRIST 72 99% SpO2
Wearable Threshold Monitor Sampling at 1 Hz in background. Dashboard stays dormant until threshold breach.
Mode 02 // Computer Vision Touchless & Hands-Free

Touchless Hand-Gesture Activation (OpenCV & MediaPipe)

Powered by Python, OpenCV, and MediaPipe, astronauts can wake and unlock the health interface completely hands-free simply by opening and closing their hand in front of the camera.
